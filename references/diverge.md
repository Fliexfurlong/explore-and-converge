# Diverge

Read when existing directions repeat, premises remain unexamined, or new mechanisms are needed. Choose the method most likely to address the sticking point. Do not inject randomness without a sticking point.

## Choose a method

| Method | When useful | Effective execution |
|---|---|---|
| Free association | Material is scattered and unorganized | Collect before scoring. The user or assistant may contribute; identify sources and assumptions. |
| Assumption inversion | A default premise seems inevitable | Extract a key assumption, form a defensible opposite rather than a straw man, and identify real applicability conditions. Verify claims about existing examples; mark unsupported cases as hypotheses or set them aside. |
| Provocation | Plausible variants still fail to open space | Temporarily use an unrealistic setting, extract a transferable principle, then return to real conditions. The setting is neither a fact nor an approved requirement. |
| Reverse / worst-case brainstorming | Positive approaches are too conservative | Imagine how to cause failure, identify the failure mechanism, then infer design opportunities. Do more than replace words with their opposites. |
| Structural analogy | Thinking stays within one domain | Describe actors, flows, and bottlenecks. Choose a distant source with a similar structure, map roles, transfer the mechanism, and explain where the mapping fails. |
| SCAMPER | A concrete idea needs systematic variants | Selectively substitute, combine, adapt, magnify or reduce, put to another use, eliminate, or rearrange. Skip operations that produce nothing useful. |
| Dimension expansion | Ideas are the same solution in different clothes | Change actors, timing, scale, mechanism, or value allocation; explain the changed axis. Scaling in opposite directions is not automatically a new mechanism. |
| Random stimulus | Familiar associations keep recurring | Use the sampler when an external stimulus is helpful. Explain the link from stimulus to mechanism and explicitly abandon forced connections. |

A structural analogy transfers how a source system addresses the corresponding bottleneck, not its aesthetics, terminology, or story. Recheck mappings that repeatedly force one role to serve several others. Do not rescue an analogy to satisfy a quota.

## Method execution references

For standard or deep exploration with a selected method, read its execution reference. Load only the method currently needed; load another when switching methods. These references add no mandatory reads to the micro route.

- [Structural analogy](methods/analogy.md): abstract the bottleneck, select a structurally relevant source, map roles, transfer the mechanism, and check mismatches and shared discoveries.
- [Assumption inversion](methods/inversion.md): identify load-bearing assumptions, form defensible opposites, check applicability conditions, and develop candidates.

## Check difference and relevance

Check core mechanisms before expanding details. Merge candidates that share a mechanism; explain how distinct routes serve the goal and which assumptions they need. Do not relist conventional baselines every time or exclude them deliberately.

Candidate count does not demonstrate sufficient divergence, and discomfort does not demonstrate quality. Briefly explain actual unproductive branches without inventing failures. Immaturity is acceptable during exploration; recommendations still need constraint and evidence checks.

For divergence only, deliver directions and open questions. If selection is also requested, hand off to comparison or seed development.
